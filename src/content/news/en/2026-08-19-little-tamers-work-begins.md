Work has begun on Little Tamers, the first strand in the Isharat series. It is a two-part course for children aged 5 to 7: the Kindergarten and Early Childhood years, and the first two years of primary school — Years 1–2, Key Stage 1 in the British system.

The idea behind the strand is to bring two levels together. The first is the elementary skill: confident use of the mouse and the keyboard. The second is the foundation of computational thinking and information literacy that grows out of that skill. Neither works without the other — skill without thinking stays mechanical, and thinking without skill has nothing to stand on.

It is built on current research: on the one hand into keeping a child safe at the computer, on the other into the cognitive development of pre-school children as they work at one. Our aim is the greatest benefit with the computer's harmful effects reduced to a minimum.

Little Tamers is being prepared in English and Arabic. Other languages are under consideration.
