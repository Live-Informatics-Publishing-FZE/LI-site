Our artists have settled the design and the format of the first book in the Little Tamers strand — _Tame the Mouse_, for children aged 5 to 6. It will be out soon.

The book follows the school year week by week. Every session is a spread the child works on directly: a short picture story first, then two activities — trace the path through a maze, match what belongs together, continue a pattern, mark the odd one out. Each session closes with Computer Time, pointing to a level in the Mouse Tamer trainer, so paper and screen move in step.

About the drawing. The child is shown a world he recognises rather than an abstract cartoon one: a family at a laptop in their own living room, a teacher among children in a classroom, the clothes and interiors of the Gulf. Warm colour, generous detail, faces you can read. The activity pages, by contrast, are kept deliberately quiet — a white ground and no ornament, so that the brightest thing on the page is the thing the child is working with.

At the end of the book there is a guide for the teacher or the parent: what each session is for, what to say, what to look out for, and what to do when there is no computer to hand.

The format is A4 with a spiral binding, so the book opens flat and a five-year-old can draw right up to the spine.

The trainer that goes with the book is free and already open. The levels are published as beta versions, so expect rough edges — but you can work with them now, and your comments are exactly what we need at this stage: [https://mouse.libooks.ae/](https://mouse.libooks.ae/) — [mail@libooks.ae](mailto:mail@libooks.ae)
