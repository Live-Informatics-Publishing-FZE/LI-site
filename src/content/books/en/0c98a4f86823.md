description: 'Every teacher knows drawing on squared paper, but few are shown how to build 40 lessons around it or analyse a child’s mistakes. Master the Grid, Part 1 is a ready-made system for children aged 5–6. It is easy to start: the first tasks are short lines on a large grid, each new task type opens with a worked example, and support fades gradually. Children copy lines and shapes from a model, draw by arrow commands and complete the symmetrical half. This develops spatial thinking, visual-motor coordination and self-regulation — the ability to act from a model and follow instructions — while arrows become a first step towards algorithms. The guide gives each lesson’s goal, steps, what to do if it doesn’t work, and answers. No special training needed.'
