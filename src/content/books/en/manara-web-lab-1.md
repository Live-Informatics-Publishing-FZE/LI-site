  The first title in the strand. A child marks up their own pages in HTML, styles
  them in CSS, and puts them online at a real address they can send to a friend.
  What they have at the end is not a folder of exercises but a small connected site
  of several pages in one style, built from start to finish by themselves.


  The work begins in an online trainer, where the result appears at once, and moves
  in stages to a real code editor on the child's own computer. Support falls away
  as it does: first the child studies a worked example, then completes one that has
  been started, then builds a project from nothing at the end of each block. The AI
  assistant is there throughout — it explains and it asks questions, and it does
  not hand over the answer.
