  Cloudburst over Paris

  Paris, as no guidebook will ever show it. Rain falls like a wall on the Rue de Rivoli. A traveler leaves a letter for Jim Morrison at Père Lachaise. Louis XIV holds court at Versailles, and in the quiet cemetery of Sainte-Geneviève-des-Bois lies the Russian Paris that history left behind. Then the road leads on to Milan, and to Istanbul, the city that never sleeps.

  Sergei Zarkov writes film novellas: short, luminous stories in which a travel diary turns into a screenplay and a letter to the dead becomes a conversation about art, love and memory. Édith Piaf meets the man who discovered her. Van Gogh, Nureyev, Bunin and Chekhov step into the frame. What the author saw and what he imagined blur into one, and Paris comes alive on every page.

  Illustrated with fourteen engravings by Alexandra Pavlova.

  For anyone who has fallen in love with Paris, or is about to.
