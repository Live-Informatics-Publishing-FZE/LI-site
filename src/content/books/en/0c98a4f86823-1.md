description: 'Every teacher knows “continue the pattern”, but it usually stops at two alternating shapes; few are shown how to build on it or why children get it wrong. Master the Pattern, Part 1 is a ready-made system of 40 lessons for children aged 5–6. It is easy to start: only one attribute changes at first, and the answer can be traced over a dotted outline. Then children find the rule by shape, colour, fill, rotation and hatching, extend a pattern forwards and backwards, read a pattern on a bracelet, track two attributes at once and an AAB rhythm. This develops inductive reasoning — spotting an attribute, generalising, transferring a rule — the basis of maths and computational thinking. The guide gives each lesson’s goal, steps, typical mistakes and answers.'
