description: 'Every teacher knows “find the odd one out”, but that is usually where it ends; few are shown how to move on to NOT, AND, OR, or why a child looks at the wrong attribute. Master the Logic, Part 1 offers 40 lessons for children aged 5–6. It is easy to start: four shapes, one differing in a single attribute; no numbers to write, and attributes are shown as icons on cards. Then children mark every shape that fits a card, sort by “yes or NOT”, ignore a distracting attribute, check two conditions at once (AND) and at least one (OR). This builds classification and logical operations — the same AND, OR and NOT that drive conditions in programming. The guide gives each lesson’s goal, steps, typical mistakes and answers.'
