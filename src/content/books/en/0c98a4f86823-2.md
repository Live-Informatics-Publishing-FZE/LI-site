description: 'Every teacher knows mazes, but a child solves an ordinary one in seconds by tracing back from the finish, and a bigger maze only adds boredom, not challenge. Master the Path, Part 1 offers 40 lessons for children aged 5–6 where the challenge lies in the rule: the obvious path is wrong. It is easy to start: the first mazes are small with a single path; children draw lightly and erase mistakes. Then they collect every star, follow a path by attribute, connect pairs, avoid tricky dead ends, choose the right order and guide two characters so they don’t block each other. This trains planning, impulse control and flexibility — the executive functions that underpin learning. The guide gives each lesson’s goal, steps, how to support a child through a hard task, and answers.'
