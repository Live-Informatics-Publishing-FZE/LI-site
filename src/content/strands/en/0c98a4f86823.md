description: 'Little Masterminds is a series of four workbooks, each in two parts — for ages 5–6 and 6–7, with 40 lessons per part. Master the Grid develops spatial thinking and visual-motor coordination and teaches working from a model and following instructions. Master the Pattern builds inductive reasoning: recognising patterns, seriation and analogies. Master the Path trains planning and self-monitoring — the executive functions that underpin learning at school. Master the Logic introduces the operations of logical thinking: attributes, classification and negation. Tasks are designed with cognitive load in mind: wordless pages, with support fading from lesson to lesson. Part 2 of each workbook comes with online practice, matched lesson by lesson. Every workbook ends with a teacher’s guide for each lesson.'
