description: 'New Ink is a series from the Qalam imprint devoted to literature being written now. It brings together prose, novellas, tales and essays by authors whose work has not yet become a classic or gathered layers of commentary. Books in the series appear in several languages: Russian, English and Arabic. Translations follow a single set of editorial standards and carry the author’s voice, not just the plot. The editions are made for close reading: short, self-contained chapters, carefully edited language, and illustrations only where they serve the text. That makes them well suited to literature and translation courses, reading groups and independent language study. New Ink is a way to take part in the literary process while it is still unfolding, rather than study it in hindsight.'
